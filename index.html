How SAENSA works

From physical signals to actionable insight.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Wireless micro-sensors ride inside crates, pallets and containers, capturing temperature, humidity, ethylene and CO₂ every few seconds.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Spiking Neural Networks process each signal as an event, at the edge, learning every product's unique respiration fingerprint.

  3. 03

    Predict

    Models forecast shelf life and decay risk hours or days before visible signs appear, with a confidence your teams can act on.

  4. 04

    Act

    Re-routing, FEFO dispatch and QA alerts flow straight into your ERP, WMS and TMS, so the right product reaches the right shelf.
